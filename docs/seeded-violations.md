# Seeded violation checklist (BEFORE variant)

The canonical mapping from the 12 seeded violations (docs/SPEC.md section 3)
to their implementations. The audit ledgers in `audit/*.json` reconcile
against this list exactly: every axe finding and manual finding on
`/before/*` must map to one row here, and nothing outside this list may be
broken.

Detection column: "axe" means the violation (or its implementation) is
caught automatically by axe-core; "manual" means it requires human judgment,
which is the point of including it.

## Reconciliation rules

- Axe-detectable seeded violations: V1, V3, V5, V6, V7 (dialog name), V8
  (button names), V9, V12 (label rule on the confirm fields) = 8 of 12,
  satisfying the >=8 requirement in docs/SPEC.md section 3.
- Manual-only seeded violations: V2, V4, V10, V11.
- The demo banner at the top of every /before page is demo chrome, is fully
  accessible, and must produce zero findings.
- Any axe finding on /before/* that does not map to a row above is a defect
  in the before-variant (an accidental extra) and must be fixed, not
  documented around.
